About ABS

Leadership
beyond sport

McGill Athletes in Business (ABS) is McGill's first student-led club dedicated to supporting varsity athletes pursuing futures in business. We exist to strengthen the student-athlete community, amplify connections, and create opportunities beyond the game.

ABS is for all athletes: past, present and future. Founded in 2025 with a kick-off session on goals, résumés and self-presentation, it has grown into a 26-person executive team across 8 portfolios.
